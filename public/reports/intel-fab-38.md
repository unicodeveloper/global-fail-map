# Intel Fab 38

The most recent and unresolved case involves semiconductor manufacturing expansion in Kiryat Gat, located in southern Shfela approximately 12 miles from the Gaza border. On June 18, 2023, Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu announced that Intel would undertake a major manufacturing expansion. By December 26–27, 2023, the Israeli government formalized the agreement, providing Intel with a \$3.2 billion grant to support a \$25 billion chip fabrication plant upgrade and expansion [[9]](https://vinnews.com/2023/12/26/intel-announces-25b-investment-in-kiryat-gat-fab/) [[10]](https://www.cnn.com/2023/12/26/tech/intel-israel-investment/index.html) [[11]](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-intel-confirms-further-15b-investment-in-kiryat-gat-1001466126).

This was Israel's largest foreign investment commitment to a private company [[10]](https://www.cnn.com/2023/12/26/tech/intel-israel-investment/index.html). Intel's Fab 28 in Kiryat Gat was already the city's largest employer, with approximately 3,500 employees [[6]](https://industrialurbanism.sites.tau.ac.il/kiryat-gat-c1eyl) [[7]](https://industrialurbanism.sites.tau.ac.il/kiryat-gat-c23kv). The new Fab 38 was designed to be a state-of-the-art semiconductor production facility with planned opening in 2028, intended to operate through at least 2035 [[12]](https://www.nytimes.com/2023/12/27/world/middleeast/intel-israel-factory-expansion.html).

However, by mid-2024, the expansion was frozen [[15]](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-intel-kiryat-gat-expansion-frozen-for-foreseeable-future-1001515435). The company announced that construction progress was being "adjusted to business developments and market conditions," a euphemism for suspension [[15]](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-intel-kiryat-gat-expansion-frozen-for-foreseeable-future-1001515435). The first clean room structure was completed, with cooling and power systems installed, but machinery installation has not proceeded [[15]](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-intel-kiryat-gat-expansion-frozen-for-foreseeable-future-1001515435). A second planned clean room has an unknown fate [[15]](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-intel-kiryat-gat-expansion-frozen-for-foreseeable-future-1001515435). Contractor workforce was reduced from 5,500 to approximately 2,000; foreign worker visas (3,000 authorized from India, Sri Lanka, and Thailand) were returned early [[15]](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-intel-kiryat-gat-expansion-frozen-for-foreseeable-future-1001515435).

By July 2025, the stalling deepened into potential collapse. Intel announced broad global layoffs (15–20 percent of workforce) and initiated major restructuring under CEO Lip-Bu Tan [[13]](https://www.inspire2rise.com/intel-plans-hundreds-of-layoffs-in-israel.html). In Israel specifically, hundreds of employees at Fab 28 faced layoffs, including middle management and automation specialists [[13]](https://www.inspire2rise.com/intel-plans-hundreds-of-layoffs-in-israel.html) [[17]](https://www.ynetnews.com/business/article/hjfn7pcbgl) [[18]](https://semiwiki.com/forum/threads/intel%E2%80%99s-israeli-fab-in-kiryat-gat-targeted-in-latest-layoff-round.23013/) [[19]](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/bjuzok00mxe). More dramatically, internal discussions began about possible closure of Fab 28 itself—the existing, operational 3,500-person facility [[13]](https://www.inspire2rise.com/intel-plans-hundreds-of-layoffs-in-israel.html) [[14]](https://www.tomshardware.com/pc-components/cpus/more-intel-layoffs-are-hitting-this-time-in-israel-company-is-even-considering-dramatic-decision-to-shut-down-fab-28) [[16]](https://www.trendforce.com/news/2025/07/08/news-intel-reportedly-began-job-cuts-on-monday-in-u-s-and-israel-eyes-possible-kiryat-gat-plant-closure/). The expansion's fate and the original fab's continuity both became uncertain.

As of September 2026, the project remains suspended. Intel has not formally cancelled the expansion, but neither has it resumed meaningful construction or workforce buildup [[15]](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-intel-kiryat-gat-expansion-frozen-for-foreseeable-future-1001515435). The company states commitment while implementing contraction. This is distinct from the older railway cases: it is not yet a completed abandonment but a stalled, unresolved commitment. A \$25 billion facility promised for 2028 operation now has no credible timeline. The 3,500 existing jobs at Fab 28 face elimination while planned new jobs (estimated in the thousands) remain unfunded and unscheduled.

**Status:** Frozen/suspended (mid-2024–present); formally neither cancelled nor fully operational. **Geographic connection:** Kiryat Gat, southern Shfela; Fab 28 currently operational; Fab 38 under construction with machinery installation suspended. **What ended:** Construction progress; workforce expansion; near-term manufacturing timeline. **Why:** Global semiconductor market downturn; Intel strategic reassessment; CEO-driven restructuring and cost reduction. **What survived:** Fab 28 operates with Intel 7 process technology; company states "full commitment" to project while implementing freezes. (Note: This project distinguishes between organization crisis—Intel's restructuring—and technology persistence—Fab 28 semiconductor production continues, though threatened with closure.)
