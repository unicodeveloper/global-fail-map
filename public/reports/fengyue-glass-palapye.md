# The Fengyue glass plant

## What Was Built and Why It Failed

Between 2007 and 2014, the Botswana Development Corporation (BDC), the government's principal development finance agency [[12]](http://www.bdc.bw/botswana-development-corporation), invested \$500 million in the Fengyue Glass Manufacturing Plant in Palapye town [[9]](https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/76350). The glass plant was conceived as a major industrial anchor for the region, intended to process raw materials into value-added products and create employment in a diversified economy beyond mining and diamonds. The plant was developed by a Chinese manufacturer, Fengyue Glass Company, and financed through government development funds.

The project was abandoned after severe cost and time overruns rendered it financially unviable. By the time construction halted in 2014, the plant had consumed substantial capital with minimal operational output. Government records show that BDC funded the project with 447.84 million Pula (approximately \$316 million at 2023 rates), with recoveries on liquidation amounting to only 67.8 million Pula [[9]](https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/76350). This resulted in a confirmed government loss of approximately 380 million Pula, or roughly \$268 million in unrecovered investment [[9]](https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/76350).

## Status and Current Disposition

The plant was liquidated, and its assets were offered for disposal. As of November 2023, the BDC issued a request for proposals (RFP) for asset recovery, indicating that the facility remained under government ownership and management through the development corporation [[9]](https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/76350). No operational production has been documented since abandonment in 2014. The precise abandonment date was not specified in available records, but the facility is no longer functional and does not appear in current industrial production statistics for Botswana.

## Causes of Failure

The plant's collapse was attributed to time and budget overruns that accumulated to unsustainable levels. Specific technical or market failures were not documented in the available sources. Instead, the evidence points to project implementation breakdown: the costs escalated beyond the original budget estimates, construction schedules slipped by years, and the operational timeline shifted repeatedly. Such pattern matches broader systemic failures in Botswana's infrastructure delivery, where multiple government projects from the 2007–2014 period experienced contractor cash flow problems, inadequate project monitoring, and poor planning [[7]](https://allafrica.com/stories/201608160686.html) [[8]](https://mphoeng.com/2021/03/22/ppps-just-what-the-doctor-ordered-for-botswanas-procurement-challenges/).

## Geographic Location

The Fengyue Glass Plant was located within Palapye town proper, making it distinct from the industrial clusters at Morupule (power station and colliery), which lie approximately 14 kilometers to the west [[22]](https://www.afdb.org/sites/default/files/documents/environmental-and-social-assessments/esia_ex_summary_morupule_b_final-22_june09.pdf).
