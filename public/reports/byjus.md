# Byju's

## What Was Attempted

Byju Raveendran, a self-taught mathematics educator, founded Byju's in 2011 in Bangalore as an online education platform offering personalized learning programmes for students preparing for competitive entrance exams (primarily the Indian Institutes of Technology entrance exam and medical school entrance exams). [[15]](https://www.voanews.com/a/india-ed-tech-firm-byju-s-founder-faces-reckoning-as-startup-implodes/7707631.html) [[16]](https://www.reuters.com/technology/indian-ed-tech-giant-byjus-faces-total-shutdown-if-insolvency-proceeds-ceo-says-2024-07-19/) [[17]](https://www.reuters.com/world/india/indian-ed-tech-giant-byjus-insolvency-case-fans-fears-employees-2024-08-22/) The platform used video lectures, interactive animations, and adaptive algorithms to personalize learning paths for each student.

## Explosive Growth and Peak Valuation

Byju's experienced explosive growth, particularly during the COVID-19 pandemic (2020–2021) when in-person schooling closed and demand for online learning surged:

- **Founded**: 2011 in Bangalore [[15]](https://www.voanews.com/a/india-ed-tech-firm-byju-s-founder-faces-reckoning-as-startup-implodes/7707631.html)
- **Peak valuation**: \$22 billion (late 2022), making Byju's the world's most valuable edtech company and one of India's most valuable startups (a "unicorn" valued above \$1 billion). [[15]](https://www.voanews.com/a/india-ed-tech-firm-byju-s-founder-faces-reckoning-as-startup-implodes/7707631.html) [[19]](https://techfundingnews.com/spotlight-on-byjus-founder-from-fortune-to-bankruptcy-a-2-2b-tragedy/)
- **User base**: At its peak, Byju's claimed millions of active students across India and international markets. [[15]](https://www.voanews.com/a/india-ed-tech-firm-byju-s-founder-faces-reckoning-as-startup-implodes/7707631.html)
- **Funding**: Byju's raised approximately \$6 billion from prominent global investors including Prosus, General Atlantic, and others. [[13]](https://www.indiatoday.in/business/story/byjus-shutdown-insolvency-proceedings-ceo-byju-raveendran-2569265-2024-07-19) [[16]](https://www.reuters.com/technology/indian-ed-tech-giant-byjus-faces-total-shutdown-if-insolvency-proceeds-ceo-says-2024-07-19/)

## Geographic Connection

Byju's maintained its headquarters and operational center in Bangalore. In April 2024, Indian regulatory authorities raided Byju's Bangalore offices, seizing laptops and documents as part of investigations into the company's sales practices and financial conduct. [[18]](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/bankruptcy-law/star-founder-broke-down-in-tears-as-crises-engulfed-byjus) Legal proceedings were filed in the High Court of Karnataka in July 2024.

## Why It Mattered

By 2022, Byju's represented the flagship success story of India's edtech boom. The company symbolized how Indian entrepreneurs could build globally competitive technology platforms and scale them to serve hundreds of millions of students. Byju's expansion into international markets (Southeast Asia, USA) suggested that Indian edtech could compete against established American players like Coursera and Khan Academy.

## What Ended and Why

Byju's collapsed from \$22 billion valuation to **below \$2 billion** (mid-2024) due to a convergence of operational, financial, and governance failures:

#### Operational Failures

1. **Aggressive, controversial sales tactics**: Byju's sales representatives employed high-pressure tactics, often targeting economically disadvantaged families, selling annual subscriptions costing \$100–500 USD through installment plans. [[15]](https://www.voanews.com/a/india-ed-tech-firm-byju-s-founder-faces-reckoning-as-startup-implodes/7707631.html) [[16]](https://www.reuters.com/technology/indian-ed-tech-giant-byjus-faces-total-shutdown-if-insolvency-proceeds-ceo-says-2024-07-19/) [[19]](https://techfundingnews.com/spotlight-on-byjus-founder-from-fortune-to-bankruptcy-a-2-2b-tragedy/) [[20]](https://www.opindia.com/2024/09/byjus-from-edtech-unicorn-to-bankruptcy-the-meteoric-rise-and-the-dramatic-fall-of-an-industry-giant/)

2. **Misalignment between marketing spend and revenue**: Byju's spent heavily on advertising (including a \$19 million sponsorship of Indian Premier League cricket and ICC cricket tournaments through the Board of Control for Cricket in India—BCCI) [[13]](https://www.indiatoday.in/business/story/byjus-shutdown-insolvency-proceedings-ceo-byju-raveendran-2569265-2024-07-19) [[14]](https://www.business-standard.com/companies/start-ups/byju-s-faces-total-shutdown-if-insolvency-proceeds-ceo-raveendran-124071901007_1.html) while revenues stagnated relative to burn rate. [[15]](https://www.voanews.com/a/india-ed-tech-firm-byju-s-founder-faces-reckoning-as-startup-implodes/7707631.html)

3. **Market saturation and churn**: The company acquired users rapidly but struggled with retention. Churn rates exceeded new-user acquisition rates by 2023, leading to declining active user numbers.

4. **Inadequate pedagogical differentiation**: While Byju's innovative learning animations were initially compelling, competitors offered similar features at lower cost or through free models, eroding Byju's value proposition.

#### Financial Failures

1. **Unprofitable unit economics**: Despite high revenue (claimed \$500+ million annually in 2022), Byju's could not achieve profitability. Operating losses exceeded \$400 million annually by 2023. [[16]](https://www.reuters.com/technology/indian-ed-tech-giant-byjus-faces-total-shutdown-if-insolvency-proceeds-ceo-says-2024-07-19/)

2. **Rapid cash burn**: The company's cash reserves depleted as operating losses accumulated. Without continued venture funding, the company faced insolvency.

3. **Inability to service debt**: Byju's defaulted on the \$19 million BCCI sponsorship payment, a public indication of financial distress.

#### Governance and Leadership Failures

1. **Poor financial controls**: Investigations uncovered misallocation of funds, unauthorized transactions, and inadequate accounting oversight. [[15]](https://www.voanews.com/a/india-ed-tech-firm-byju-s-founder-faces-reckoning-as-startup-implodes/7707631.html) [[18]](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/bankruptcy-law/star-founder-broke-down-in-tears-as-crises-engulfed-byjus)

2. **Founder overreach**: CEO Byju Raveendran made major strategic decisions (international expansion, large acquisitions of other edtech companies) without adequate board oversight or financial discipline. [[15]](https://www.voanews.com/a/india-ed-tech-firm-byju-s-founder-faces-reckoning-as-startup-implodes/7707631.html) [[18]](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/bankruptcy-law/star-founder-broke-down-in-tears-as-crises-engulfed-byjus)

3. **Departure of experienced executives**: Key executives left the company, citing governance concerns and unrealistic growth targets.

## Insolvency Proceedings and Uncertain Status

In July 2024, lenders initiated insolvency proceedings against Byju's under India's Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code. [[13]](https://www.indiatoday.in/business/story/byjus-shutdown-insolvency-proceedings-ceo-byju-raveendran-2569265-2024-07-19) [[14]](https://www.business-standard.com/companies/start-ups/byju-s-faces-total-shutdown-if-insolvency-proceeds-ceo-raveendran-124071901007_1.html) [[15]](https://www.voanews.com/a/india-ed-tech-firm-byju-s-founder-faces-reckoning-as-startup-implodes/7707631.html) Byju Raveendran petitioned the High Court of Karnataka to quash the insolvency proceedings, arguing that the company could resolve its financial obligations if given time. [[14]](https://www.business-standard.com/companies/start-ups/byju-s-faces-total-shutdown-if-insolvency-proceeds-ceo-raveendran-124071901007_1.html)

**Status as of August 2, 2024**: The National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT) in Bangalore **quashed the insolvency proceedings** after Byju Raveendran's co-founder brother, Riju Ravindran, agreed to pay pending creditor dues (reportedly related to the BCCI sponsorship and other liabilities). [[21]](https://www.reuters.com/world/india/facing-insolvency-indias-byjus-hit-with-new-challenge-lenders-2024-08-02/)

**Operational status (as of September 2026)**: The sources reviewed do not document the company's current operational status, employee levels, or platform functionality following the insolvency quashing. Available evidence confirms the company faced operational challenges and significantly reduced staff (from ~27,000 employees and ~16,000 teaching staff at peak to unknown current levels) but does not confirm whether Byju's ceased operations entirely or continues in reduced form.

## What Survived

The Byju's technology platform itself survives in some form, but its market position and valuation have been devastated. The edtech model of video-based personalized learning persists (both at Byju's and at competing platforms), but Byju's specific platform no longer commands premium pricing or market dominance. The company's investors (Prosus, General Atlantic, and others) lost substantial sums—Prosus alone reportedly lost \$800 million. [[15]](https://www.voanews.com/a/india-ed-tech-firm-byju-s-founder-faces-reckoning-as-startup-implodes/7707631.html)
