# Solucion Vial Guayasamin

## The Vision and Cost

Between 2016 and 2019, Quito's municipal government initiated one of Ecuador's largest urban mobility projects: a \$131 million tunnel and arterial road system designed to connect Quito's northern and eastern zones with the valleys of Tumbaco and Cumbayá, bypassing congested surface streets. The **Solución Vial Guayasamín** was conceived as a strategic infrastructure intervention that would reshape regional mobility for an estimated 200,000+ commuters using the Guayasamín corridor daily .

## Project Scope and Geography

The project centered on a depressed underpass and tunnel system along Avenida Oswaldo Guayasamín, extending from La Primavera in Cumbayá (eastern valley) through interconnectors to the south of Quito. The scope included grade-separated intersections, a novel architectural feature for Quito at the time. The primary contractor was **China Road and Bridges Corporation (CRBC)**, a division of the state-owned Chinese conglomerate, signaling Ecuador's dependence on external financing for major infrastructure [[8]](https://www.primicias.ec/quito/municipio-solucion-vial-guayasamin-crbc-arbitraje-tumbaco-cumbaya-106555/).

## The Failure: Absolute Nullity and Contract Cancellation

On **September 19, 2025**, an international arbitration tribunal ruled the Solución Vial Guayasamín contract **absolutely null and void**, protecting Quito from a \$40 million payment claim that CRBC had filed against the municipality . The international arbitration panel determined that the contract had been vitiated from its inception—meaning the foundational legal agreement itself was defective—through a combination of factors:

- **Unilateral design modifications** without prior citizen consultation or formal amendment protocols
- **Change-order disputes** between the municipality and CRBC regarding scope, timing, and cost responsibility
- **Procedural violations** in contract administration and environmental/social review processes

The arbitration decision confirmed that the city had no contractual obligation to complete the project or compensate the contractor for work already performed. This represented a decisive administrative cancellation with full legal closure .

## What Was Lost and What Survived

The cancellation eliminated:
- A planned \$131 million eastern mobility corridor
- Approximately 8.5 kilometers of new tunnel/grade-separated infrastructure
- Estimated 30-year reduction of 25-30% in commute times for eastern valley residents
- Employment for several hundred construction workers over the 4-5 year planned execution period

What survived the failure:
- The existing surface-level Avenida Oswaldo Guayasamín remains in service (rehabbing as of 2025-2026)
- CRBC's parent entity, **China Communications Construction Company (CCCC)**, maintains other contracts in Ecuador
- The arbitration ruling itself became a legal precedent limiting similar dispute outcomes in the region

**Status as of September 2026:** The Solución Vial Guayasamín is permanently **CANCELLED**. No alternative corridor project has been funded or initiated by the municipality .
