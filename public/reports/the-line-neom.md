# The Line

## The Vision

In January 2021, Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman unveiled *The Line* as the centerpiece of Neom. The project, designed by Morphosis architects, would be a linear city stretching 170 kilometers (105 miles) along the Red Sea coast, contained within a structure just 200 meters (656 feet) wide and 500 meters (1,640 feet) tall. The city would house 9 million residents in a single, unbroken corridor of vertical living, and would cost \$500 billion to complete. [[3]](https://ig.ft.com/saudi-neom-line/) [[2]](https://www.independent.co.uk/news/world/middle-east/saudi-arabia-neom-the-line-mohammed-bin-salman-b2908541.html) [[8]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neom)

The architectural proposition was radical: a complete rejection of traditional urban sprawl in favor of perfect linear density. All residents would live within walking distance of a central axis containing transportation, commerce, and culture. The city was designed to be entirely car-free, with the entire population theoretically able to walk from one end of the city to the other in under 20 minutes. [[8]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neom) The design promised radical sustainability, with zero-gravity streets, artificial intelligence managing all systems, and energy generation from renewable sources. [[2]](https://www.independent.co.uk/news/world/middle-east/saudi-arabia-neom-the-line-mohammed-bin-salman-b2908541.html)

## Financial Collapse and Scaling Down

By late 2025, the project had been suspended indefinitely, and the gap between vision and reality had widened into a chasm. As of September 2025, after spending more than \$50 billion, only 2.4 kilometers of foundation had been completed. [[20]](https://vision2030.ai/analysis/the-line-cost-per-kilometre/) An internal audit presented to Neom's board in spring 2024 calculated the true cost to complete The Line at approximately \$8.8 trillion, with completion extending to 2080. [[19]](https://medium.com/@TheCapitalReview/the-long-unravelling-of-the-line-76165a67b164) [[20]](https://vision2030.ai/analysis/the-line-cost-per-kilometre/)

The scaling trajectory reveals the successive recalibration of ambition:

| Phase | Year | Planned Population | Planned Length | Status |
|-------|------|-------------------|-----------------|--------|
| Original vision | 2021 | 9 million | 170 km | Announced |
| First revision | 2023 | 1.5 million | 100 km | Internal adjustment |
| Second revision | 2025 | 600,000 | 50 km | Acknowledged |
| Third revision | 2025 | 300,000 | Unknown | Current (paused) |

[[5]](https://www.independent.co.uk/news/world/middle-east/the-line-saudi-arabia-megacity-neom-b2858056.html) [[3]](https://ig.ft.com/saudi-neom-line/) [[23]](https://vision2030.ai/encyclopedia/neom/)

The cost implications were staggering. The Channel Tunnel, one of the world's most expensive per-kilometer infrastructure projects, cost approximately \$269 million per kilometer to build. [[16]](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/operations/our-insights/learn-from-the-past-build-for-the-future-saudi-arabias-new-city-on-the-red-sea) The Line, as executed, had cost \$20.8 billion per kilometer—a figure seventy-seven times higher. [[20]](https://vision2030.ai/analysis/the-line-cost-per-kilometre/)

## Financial Manipulation and Governance Failure

In March 2025, the Wall Street Journal gained access to Neom's internal audit, conducted by PIF's internal reviewers. The audit documented systematic financial misrepresentation. Management, assisted by consulting firm McKinsey, had relied on "unrealistically rosy assumptions" to justify mounting costs. More seriously, the audit found "evidence of deliberate manipulation of finances" by certain members of management. [[21]](https://www.fastcompany.com/91481217/saudi-arabias-the-line-is-collapsing-into-a-hyphen) [[22]](https://houseofsaud.com/neom-2026-the-line-saudi-arabia-status-update/)

One specific instance typified the problem: revenue projections for residential units were inflated by 3.8 times through spreadsheet adjustments that bore no relationship to market research or comparable projects. [[19]](https://medium.com/@TheCapitalReview/the-long-unravelling-of-the-line-76165a67b164) [[22]](https://houseofsaud.com/neom-2026-the-line-saudi-arabia-status-update/) Rather than accepting the revised figures and scaling the project to match available capital, leadership instead scaled the project downward only after the divergence between projection and reality became impossible to conceal.

## What Remains

As of September 2026, The Line exists as an indefinitely paused project within Neom. Official communications describe it as a "multi-generational development," language that obscures the absence of any construction timeline, funding commitment, or architectural revision. [[23]](https://vision2030.ai/encyclopedia/neom/) The 2.4 kilometers of foundation sits largely undisturbed, with "deep trenches stretching across the landscape" visible in satellite imagery. [[1]](https://gizmodo.com/saudi-arabias-dystopian-futuristic-city-project-is-crashing-and-burning-2000683752)

The suspension does not represent cancellation. Neom continues to operate as an entity, with other components advancing: Oxagon, an industrial manufacturing zone, remains under development; Trojena, a ski resort project, continues at reduced scale; and a green hydrogen production facility is reportedly 90 percent complete. [[23]](https://vision2030.ai/encyclopedia/neom/) The Line, however, has transitioned from construction project to stranded infrastructure—a monument to geometric ambition meeting financial reality.
