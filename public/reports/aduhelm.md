## Approved, then commercially discontinued

Aduhelm, the Alzheimer's medicine aducanumab, received FDA accelerated approval on June 7, 2021. On January 31, 2024, Biogen announced that it would discontinue development and commercialisation and terminate the ENVISION confirmatory study. The company explicitly said that this decision was not related to new safety or efficacy concerns. [[1]](https://investors.biogen.com/news-releases/news-release-details/biogen-realign-resources-alzheimers-disease-franchise) [[2]](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/appletter/2021/761178Orig1s000ltr.pdf)

Those are different statuses at different points in the story. Accelerated approval did not mean all questions about clinical benefit had been resolved. Commercial discontinuation was not a safety recall. This entry records Biogen's dated programme decision without inventing a separate FDA withdrawal date. [[1]](https://investors.biogen.com/news-releases/news-release-details/biogen-realign-resources-alzheimers-disease-franchise) [[2]](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/appletter/2021/761178Orig1s000ltr.pdf)

## The promise and the evidence problem

Aducanumab is an antibody aimed at aggregated amyloid-beta in the brain. Biogen's pivotal development programme included two large Phase 3 studies, EMERGE and ENGAGE. Their clinical results were discordant: one supported a treatment benefit in its high-dose group, while the other did not meet its primary endpoint. Those differences became central to the debate over how the data should be interpreted. [[3]](https://www.jpreventionalzheimer.com/6775-investigating-partially-discordant-results-in-phase-3-studies-of-aducanumab.html) [[4]](https://alz-journals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/alz.047259)

The FDA used its accelerated approval pathway, based on an effect on amyloid plaques rather than a completed demonstration that the treatment produced the anticipated clinical benefit. The approval letter required further study to verify that benefit. That is the key evidential distinction, not simply whether a drug could be said to have cleared a regulatory threshold. [[2]](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/appletter/2021/761178Orig1s000ltr.pdf)

## The study intended to resolve the uncertainty

ENVISION, registered as **NCT05310071**, was the post-marketing study intended to verify aducanumab's clinical benefit. Biogen published an update on its design in January 2022. The study was randomized and placebo-controlled, addressing the remaining question with clinical outcomes rather than treating plaque reduction as the final answer. [[5]](https://investors.biogen.com/news-releases/news-release-details/update-phase-4-envision-confirmatory-study-aduhelmr) [[6]](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05310071)

When Biogen terminated the programme in 2024, ENVISION did not deliver the intended completed confirmatory test. That limits what the discontinuation can tell us. A company ending a study for portfolio reasons is not the same as a completed study demonstrating futility, and the announcement should not be rewritten as one. [[1]](https://investors.biogen.com/news-releases/news-release-details/biogen-realign-resources-alzheimers-disease-franchise) [[6]](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05310071)

## Why Biogen ended the programme

Biogen described a strategic review that began in January 2023. It considered the time and investment required for ENVISION and how the treatment landscape might change before a potential traditional approval. The company also sought partners or external financing and said it had not identified a suitable route. [[1]](https://investors.biogen.com/news-releases/news-release-details/biogen-realign-resources-alzheimers-disease-franchise)

Resources were instead to support other Alzheimer's programmes, including Leqembi with Eisai and investigational approaches targeting tau. This is the company's stated rationale. It provides a direct explanation for the allocation decision without proving the relative weight of every commercial, payer or scientific pressure around Aduhelm. [[1]](https://investors.biogen.com/news-releases/news-release-details/biogen-realign-resources-alzheimers-disease-franchise)

The programme had also attracted public scrutiny. A December 2022 congressional investigation examined the FDA review process and Biogen's launch planning. That record is part of the institutional history, but scrutiny itself should not be substituted for the clinical evidence or treated as a quantified cause of the later discontinuation. [[7]](https://oversightdemocrats.house.gov/news/press-releases/maloney-and-pallone-release-staff-report-on-review-approval-and-pricing-of)

## What remained

Biogen said it had terminated its licence from Neurimmune and that rights to aducanumab would revert to that company. Commercial withdrawal therefore did not erase the underlying antibody, intellectual property or prior trial data. It changed who could decide what, if anything, to do with them next. [[1]](https://investors.biogen.com/news-releases/news-release-details/biogen-realign-resources-alzheimers-disease-franchise)

The unresolved issues are substantive. Discordant trials require explanation; a biomarker's relationship to clinical benefit must be tested; and an accelerated approval can create obligations whose fulfilment takes years. No single commercial decision supplies answers to all three.

## Location and confidence

The pin marks Biogen's headquarters in Cambridge, Massachusetts, the organisation announcing the discontinuation. It is not a trial-site pin and does not locate every patient enrolled in the global studies. [[1]](https://investors.biogen.com/news-releases/news-release-details/biogen-realign-resources-alzheimers-disease-franchise)

Confidence is high in the approval date, January 31, 2024 programme decision and ENVISION's intended role, supported by FDA, company and registry records. The case illustrates the distance between a regulatory opening and a durable, fully evidenced treatment programme. Its ending should remain as precisely labelled as its beginning.
