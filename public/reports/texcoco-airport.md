## An airport stopped during construction

Mexico City's proposed new international airport at Lake Texcoco was already under construction when the incoming president announced that it would be cancelled. Andrés Manuel López Obrador made that announcement on October 29, 2018, after a public consultation organised by his transition team. [[1]](https://www.bbc.com/news/world-latin-america-46026860) [[2]](https://www.reuters.com/article/us-mexico-airport/what-is-next-for-mexico-city-airport-after-mega-project-axed-idUSKCN1N51BQ/)

The status is a cancelled infrastructure project. It is not an airport that opened and later closed, and it is not the same facility as Felipe Ángeles International Airport, which was built elsewhere. The pin marks the Texcoco construction site, northeast of central Mexico City. [[1]](https://www.bbc.com/news/world-latin-america-46026860) [[3]](https://mexicocity.cdmx.gob.mx/venues/felipe-angeles-airport/?lang=en)

## The attempt

The project was intended to address the capital region's airport-capacity constraints with a major new hub. Announced under President Enrique Peña Nieto in 2014, it combined a large terminal, runways and supporting infrastructure on the former lakebed. By the time of the 2018 decision, the construction and financing commitments were substantial. [[1]](https://www.bbc.com/news/world-latin-america-46026860) [[2]](https://www.reuters.com/article/us-mexico-airport/what-is-next-for-mexico-city-airport-after-mega-project-axed-idUSKCN1N51BQ/)

Building on the lakebed posed real geotechnical questions. Research on the former Lake Texcoco describes soils and groundwater behaviour that make the location unusually demanding. That is evidence of engineering complexity, not proof that the project was physically impossible to complete. [[4]](https://www.degruyterbrill.com/document/doi/10.1515/geo-2019-0010/html)

## The decision came before the new presidency

The October consultation favoured an alternative airport strategy. It was not a national election conducted by Mexico's electoral institute, and its result was not a legally binding nationwide referendum. López Obrador nevertheless committed to follow it. The distinction prevents the political decision from being given a legal status it did not possess. [[5]](https://cdn.aglty.io/scotia-bank-mexico/pdf/emp-gob/guia-economica/2018/naim261018.pdf) [[6]](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2018/10/29/mexico-referendum-rejects-part-built-14bn-airport-project)

October 29 is the announcement date used in this atlas. The later administrative steps, construction wind-down and contract settlements were not all completed that day. An incoming government's commitment to cancel and the legal-financial process of ending a megaproject are different events. [[1]](https://www.bbc.com/news/world-latin-america-46026860) [[2]](https://www.reuters.com/article/us-mexico-airport/what-is-next-for-mexico-city-airport-after-mega-project-axed-idUSKCN1N51BQ/)

## Why it ended

The cancellation followed a change in political leadership and a dispute over cost, location and the available alternatives. Supporters argued that abandoning work already under way would waste investment and leave the original capacity problem unresolved. Opponents argued for a different approach to meeting the region's aviation needs. [[2]](https://www.reuters.com/article/us-mexico-airport/what-is-next-for-mexico-city-airport-after-mega-project-axed-idUSKCN1N51BQ/) [[7]](https://imco.org.mx/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/Bolet%C3%ADn_NAIM-Indispensable.pdf)

Those positions cannot be reduced to a single uncontested cost number. Estimated construction cost, financing obligations, cancellation payments and the value of unfinished assets measure different things. This report does not treat a secondary account of alleged irregularities as an established finding of criminal responsibility.

The narrower, well-supported explanation is that the incoming administration chose not to continue the Texcoco plan and pursued a different airport strategy. The public decision was political and economic; it was not announced as the inevitable consequence of one conclusively demonstrated engineering defect. [[1]](https://www.bbc.com/news/world-latin-america-46026860) [[2]](https://www.reuters.com/article/us-mexico-airport/what-is-next-for-mexico-city-airport-after-mega-project-axed-idUSKCN1N51BQ/)

## A different landscape

The alternative Felipe Ángeles airport is a separate location and project. Mexico City's official visitor information identifies it as such. Its existence does not mean the Texcoco terminal was completed under another name. [[3]](https://mexicocity.cdmx.gob.mx/venues/felipe-angeles-airport/?lang=en)

The former lake area was subsequently associated with ecological restoration and public space. The Mexican presidency records the inauguration of the Lake Texcoco Ecological Park in 2024. The landscape's later use belongs in the story because cancellation did not freeze the site at the moment construction stopped. [[8]](https://www.gob.mx/presidencia/prensa/presidente-amlo-inaugura-parque-ecologico-lago-de-texcoco-14-mil-hectareas-recuperadas-en-beneficio-del-valle-de-mexico?idiom=es)

That does not settle every dispute about the airport decision, nor does it erase the money, materials and contractual commitments already involved. It records what the public authority chose to build and restore next.

## Location and confidence

The pin is a project-site marker on the Lake Texcoco construction area, not a ministry headquarters or the later Felipe Ángeles airport. Confidence is high in the 2018 cancellation announcement and in the distinction between the two airports. [[1]](https://www.bbc.com/news/world-latin-america-46026860) [[3]](https://mexicocity.cdmx.gob.mx/venues/felipe-angeles-airport/?lang=en)

The exact final net cost of cancellation depends on accounting scope and subsequent settlements. Rather than invent a definitive figure, the report keeps that question open. The enduring lesson is an inference: physical progress alone does not give a megaproject durable political legitimacy.
