# Ferrocarriles del Ecuador

## A Collapsed National System

The railway network inherited from Ecuador's early 20th-century development—highlighted by the arrival of the **Ferrocarril del Sur** at Chimbacalle station in Quito on **June 25, 1908**—had by 2020 become a financial liability consuming government subsidies with minimal operational revenue .

The **Empresa de Ferrocarriles del Ecuador** (public railway enterprise) operated approximately **965.6 kilometers** of track at its peak, connecting coastal (Guayaquil) and highland (Quito) zones through mountainous terrain. The system required specialized engineering: the line navigated Cotopaxi volcano terrain and Andean passes exceeding 4,000 meters elevation [[18]](https://ecuadorexplorer.com/html/train.html).

## The Liquidation: May 2020

On **May 16, 2020**, President **Lenín Moreno** issued **Decree 1057**, ordering the immediate liquidation and extinction of Ferrocarriles del Ecuador as a state enterprise . On June 16, 2020, a follow-up decree transferred remaining assets to the Tourism Ministry with a mandate to preserve heritage operations [[29]](https://www.elcomercio.com/opinion/enrique-echeverria-ferrocarril-guayaquil-quito/).

**Operational collapse indicators** preceding the decree:
- Accumulated financial losses: \$144 million (2012–September 2020) [[21]](https://vialibre-ffe.com/pdf/8436_Mercado_Ferroviario_Ecuador.pdf)
- Workforce reduction: 325 workers cut to 103 by liquidation date [[20]](https://www.primicias.ec/quito/rehabilitacion-estacion-tren-chimbacalle-derrame-combustible-talleres-chiriyacu-historia-empresa-liquidacion-110509/)
- Cash position at liquidation: \$1,000 in reserves [[21]](https://vialibre-ffe.com/pdf/8436_Mercado_Ferroviario_Ecuador.pdf)
- Revenue dysfunction: Passenger subsidies exceeded farebox recovery by 400%+ in final years
- Maintenance deferral: Track deterioration accelerated; rolling stock cannibalized for parts

## Quito's Railway Infrastructure: The Chimbacalle Complex

**Chimbacalle Station** (south Quito, Barrio de Chimbacalle, elevation ~2,800m) served as Quito's primary rail terminus from 1908 until the system's collapse. The station complex included:

- **Main passenger terminal** (1908, neocolonial architecture)
- **Locomotive workshops** (Chiriyacu complex, south of Chimbacalle)
- **Maintenance sheds** and rail yard (>50 hectares)
- **Residential compounds** for rail workers

## Chiriyacu Workshops: Abandonment and Environmental Contamination

The **Chiriyacu workshops**, located south of Chimbacalle station near the railway yard, operated as the primary maintenance facility for Ecuador's locomotive and rolling stock fleet for over a century. The complex maintained \$144+ million in accumulated state investment.

**Abandonment Status (as of November 2025):**
- **Deterioration phase:** 5+ years (since 2020)
- **Current condition:** Severe structural decay; fire-damaged roofing; corroded machinery; vandalized rolling stock
- **Fuel contamination:** A significant diesel spill in the workshops' tank area, documented in November 2025, poses environmental and remediation liability [[20]](https://www.primicias.ec/quito/rehabilitacion-estacion-tren-chimbacalle-derrame-combustible-talleres-chiriyacu-historia-empresa-liquidacion-110509/)
- **Staffing:** Minimal caretaker presence; no active maintenance operations
- **Asset salvage:** The **Instituto Nacional de Patrimonio Cultural (INPC)** evaluated remaining heritage equipment (over 100-year-old locomotives, passenger cars) beginning in late 2020. Some items were relocated to potential museum sites; others remain deteriorating on-site

**What Survived at Chiriyacu:**
- Historic locomotive collections (steam engines with 100+ year lineage) — partially preserved
- Original foundry equipment and metalworking tools — tagged for heritage documentation but not restored
- Railway engineering archives — status uncertain; some transferred to INPC custody

## Chimbacalle Station: From Abandonment to Partial Recovery

Unlike Chiriyacu, **Chimbacalle station** received renewed municipal attention starting in 2023-2024:

- **Rehabilitation Phase (2024-2025):** Structural repairs, historical restoration, and heritage interpretation installations funded by Quito's historic center recovery budget
- **Operational Restart (December 24, 2024):** A heritage railway service—using a restored **chiva ferroviaria** (railway-mounted truck)—resumed limited operations for worker inspections and heritage tourism, marking the first service in approximately 5 years
- **Current Status (2026):** Chimbacalle operates as a heritage/cultural site with periodic tourist service, not as a functional transportation node

The distinction is crucial: **Chimbacalle recovered because it was designated cultural heritage and received municipal restoration funding; Chiriyacu remained abandoned because it lacked similar protected status and faced contamination liability.**

## What Survived the Ferrocarriles Liquidation

The liquidation dissolved the state enterprise, but several assets and institutional fragments survived:

1. **Rolling Stock Heritage Preservation:** INPC recovered ~116 items (locomotives, passenger coaches, freight wagons) from various yards, including Chiriyacu, for potential museum exhibition [[24]](https://www.elcomercio.com/actualidad/bienes-ferrocarriles-ecuador-reciclados-vagones.html)

2. **Tourism Ministry Operations:** The Tourism Ministry inherited 10 diesel-electric locomotives, 7 steam locomotives, 7 autoferros (self-propelled units), and 36 passenger coaches—approximately 60 operational or restorable units [[27]](https://www.entrevias.com.uy/ecuador-ministerio-de-turismo-busca-solucion-para-ferrocarriles-del-ecuador/). As of 2026, the ministry operates limited heritage services using a fraction of this fleet

3. **Physical Infrastructure Survival:** Approximately **25 kilometers** of the 965 km network received maintenance sufficient for heritage operations (primarily the Quito-Latacunga segment) [[22]](https://www.resumenlatinoamericano.org/2020/06/10/ecuador-el-cierre-del-ferrocarril-bien-patrimonial-del-ecuador-es-anticonstitucional/). The Chimbacalle-Riobamba segment (south) remains largely inoperative, with 2024 discussions of potential restoration initiated by provincial governments [[15]](https://www.eluniverso.com/noticias/ecuador/quito-rehabilitacion-tren-daniel-noboa-ecuador-nota/)

4. **Labor and Expertise:** Of the 325 workers displaced, approximately 103 transitioned to the Tourism Ministry's heritage operations; the remainder either retired, found employment in road transport, or left the labor force
